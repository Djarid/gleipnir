**never** tool permissions or safety policy.

Home for GOTCHA's graduated Guardrails / `LESSONS.md` content. Under Gleipnir
the promotion path is **G-4c measured graduation**, not a manual 15-item cap: a
candidate graduates only if, during trial, it fired on a real event, is
associated with a measured reduction in the failure rate it was proposed
against, and stays under a false-positive threshold. Failing candidates expire.
